% 1 · LÍNEA 543 — la definición del ruido, en Métodos.
%
% Dice:
%   $X_k^{\mathrm{obs}}=X_k^{\mathrm{true}}+\varepsilon_{X,k}$, where
%   $\varepsilon_{X,k}\sim \mathcal{N}(0,\sigma_{\mathrm{obs}}^2)$, with
%   $\sigma_{\mathrm{obs}} \in \{0.01N,\;0.05N,\;0.10N\}$.
%
% Reemplazar por:
% ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

$X_k^{\mathrm{obs}}=\max\!\left(0,\;X_k^{\mathrm{true}}\,(1+\varepsilon_{X,k})\right)$,
where $\varepsilon_{X,k}\sim \mathcal{N}(0,\sigma_{\mathrm{rel}}^2)$ and
$\sigma_{\mathrm{rel}} \in \{0.05,\;0.10,\;0.20\}$. The perturbation is
multiplicative, so its magnitude scales with the observed quantity at each
time step rather than with the population size $N$. This matters because
$I(t)$ is small relative to $N$ over most of an epidemic curve: an additive
perturbation specified as a fraction of $N$ would be disproportionate
exactly where the infectious compartment carries the information the latent
function must recover. With $N=1000$ and $I_0=10$, for instance, a
perturbation of $0.01N$ equals the entire initial infectious population. The
three levels correspond to $5\%$, $10\%$ and $20\%$ relative observational
error and are intended as an interpretable robustness grid, not as
epidemiologically validated thresholds.

The behavior changes substantially when the observations used to anchor the
training windows are noisy. Across the seven scenarios and three noise
levels, mean $\mathrm{RMSE}_{\beta}$ is higher for windowed training than
for global training in 18 of the 21 scenario--noise combinations. The three
exceptions all occur at the lowest noise level and none of them is
statistically significant. No noisy condition shows a statistically
significant latent-recovery advantage for windowed training.

The reversal is already visible at the lowest noise level,
$\sigma_{\mathrm{rel}}=0.05$, in four of the seven scenarios. In the Abrupt
intervention scenario, mean $\mathrm{RMSE}_{\beta}$ increases from $0.024$
under global training to $0.043$ with $m=10$, whereas in the noise-free
experiment it had decreased from $0.00945$ to $0.00468$: the same
supervision horizon that halved the latent error with exact states nearly
doubles it once the anchors carry $5\%$ relative error. Seasonal, Two-wave
and Hospital saturation reverse at this level as well. Constant, Linear and
Tanh do not, and these are the three exceptions noted above; in all three
the difference is small and not statistically significant. At
$\sigma_{\mathrm{rel}}=0.10$ and $0.20$ the reversal is present in all seven
scenarios.

Across the complete 21-condition robustness sweep, 17 comparisons are
nominally significant under both paired tests, and all 17 are in the
direction of higher latent error for windowed training; none is significant
in the opposite direction. After Benjamini--Hochberg correction at $q=0.05$,
16 remain significant under both tests: every scenario--noise combination
except Constant at $\sigma_{\mathrm{rel}}=0.05$ and $0.10$, Linear at
$0.05$ and $0.10$, and Tanh at $0.05$.
